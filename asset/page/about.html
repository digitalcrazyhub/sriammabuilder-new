Our History

Sri Amma Industrial Developer PVT LTD

Sri Amma industrial developers pvt ltd (formerly known as SRI AMMA BUILDERS @ 1993) was incorporated in the year 2007 by Mr.OK Muthukrishnan, the first generation Entrepreneur. Today the company embarked a remarkable success in the construction field both in Industrial and Residential. Environment friendly is the motto of the company. A commitment to community welfare and environment protection continued undiminished, in fact an integral part of company policy.

On account of the changing market dynamics SAIDPL has also adapted various changes to facilitate growth and development. The accommodating way of functioning helps us to understand the exact nature of any project in a short span of time which is the most important reason for our success.
